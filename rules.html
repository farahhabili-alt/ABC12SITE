The Codex of Rules

Every delegate agrees to the following when they register.

  • Be on time: Respect the schedule, sessions, and everyone's time.
  • Attend all sessions: Attendance is mandatory unless you have a valid reason communicated to DS.
  • Respect everyone: No harassment, discrimination, bullying, inappropriate physical contact, or disrespectful behavior.
  • Respect the venue: Keep your space clean and take care of the facilities.
  • Stay safe: Alcohol and drugs are strictly prohibited. No smoking in closed spaces.
  • Wear your badge: Keep it visible throughout the conference.
  • Keep phones silent: Phone usage during sessions is not permitted, keep it on silent.
  • Take care of your keys: Keys must be handed to the room responsible, who will then keep them safe and return them to DS as instructed.
  • Enjoy the experience: Participate, connect, respect others, and make the most of the conference. And write a lot of sugar cubes.

⚠️ Important

These rules apply to everyone, regardless of their position or role. Violations may result in appropriate consequences depending on their severity.

If something goes wrong, communicate with your DS instead of handling it alone.

❤️ Most importantly

Take care of yourself. Take care of each other. Take care of the experience.

Respect the rules. Respect the people. Enjoy the conference. ✨
